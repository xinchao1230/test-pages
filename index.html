User Activation Propagation Test

Empirically verifies the browser's transient user-activation model: the ~5s lifespan, that window.open consumes activation, and that a click inside a (cross-origin) child iframe propagates activation to the top frame. Serve over http:// with the popup blocker on. Reads navigator.userActivation and detects blocked popups via window.open returning null.
