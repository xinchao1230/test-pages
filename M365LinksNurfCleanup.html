Why Remove Each Category
CategoryVariants in CodeReason for Removal
Upsell/Inform (all) 10 variants Edge-DB users don't need "use Edge" prompts. Low value, causes notification fatigue. Per product requirements.
OptOut Flyout (all) 8 variants Intrusive & verbose format. High error rates reported in telemetry. High dismiss rates. Replace with less intrusive banners.
OptOut Sidebar (all) 4 variants Experiment-specific treatment variants. Complex sidebar state dependency. Simplified to universal banner approach.
OptOut Modal (all) 3 variants Most intrusive format. "Last resort" no longer needed with 4x banner cap. Causes highest user dissatisfaction.
Extra Banner Variants 4 variants A/B testing phase complete. V1 consistently best or equal performance. Simplify to single variant per source.
